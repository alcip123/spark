Spark — feed concepts

Drawn with Spark's real tokens (Cool Modern palette from src/theme.ts), real facts from the corpus, and the real Spark mark. Light only. Cards carry no category label — that stays internal. Tabs are centered, Topics · For you · Saved, no gear. Dive Deeper has left the dock and now lives inside an opened fact.

Click any fact to expand it — click the dimmed area to close.

Concept A

Compact — density first

2–3 facts per screen, free scroll. Just the fact and two quiet actions — nothing else competing. Tap opens it.

Concept B

Tall card — focus kept

~1.2 per screen, still free scroll. The next card peeks. One fact owns the view and keeps the big text, so opening it is a smaller jump.

Concept C

Mixed — with the image slot

A, plus room to grow. Topic chips under the tabs; a fact that later carries an image gets a taller media card. Nothing here requires building images now.

Open questions

Three things this raises

Where do Settings go? The gear is gone, so the settings screen needs another door. Profile tab in the dock is the obvious one — it's already there.

The dock is down to two icons (Discover, Profile) now that Dive Deeper moved into the opened fact. Two icons may not justify a floating pill at all — worth deciding whether the dock survives this change.

Dive Deeper needs an entry per fact. Today it reads whichever fact is on screen; here it reads the fact you opened, which is actually less ambiguous — one less thing to guess at with several cards visible.

Dropping the category label means the feed shows no topic cue at all. Concept C's chips are the only place topics stay visible — worth checking that discovery doesn't suffer, since stumbling onto new categories is a stated product goal.
