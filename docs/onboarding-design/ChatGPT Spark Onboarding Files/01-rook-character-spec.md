# Rook the Raven: Definitive Character Specification

## Character role

Rook is Spark’s companion and visual representative. Rook notices fascinating ideas, quietly investigates them, and invites the user to follow.

Rook represents curiosity, intelligence, memory, discovery, observation, and the satisfaction of finding something worth knowing.

Rook is not a teacher, lecturer, game host, or guilt-based retention device.

## Personality

Rook should feel:

- Curious
- Intelligent
- Observant
- Comforting
- Warm
- Slightly mischievous
- Quietly confident
- Interested in the world
- Pleased to see the user

Rook should never feel:

- Hyperactive
- Childish
- Loud
- Sinister
- Gothic
- Smug
- Sarcastic
- Academic
- Manipulative
- Desperate for attention

The target emotional impression is:

> Rook noticed something fascinating and wants to share it with me.

## Canonical silhouette

Rook uses the selected compact cartoon direction:

- Pear-shaped body
- Large rounded head
- Head slightly wider through the upper half
- Soft asymmetry throughout the silhouette
- Three-part crown tuft with one off-center feather
- One raised lavender eyebrow feather
- Short rounded beak
- Compact folded crescent-like wings
- Tiny simplified feet
- Three-point tail visible beside the body
- Barely visible neck

Rook must remain recognizable at approximately 32 by 32 pixels. Do not depend on small feather details.

## Proportions

Use these approximate relationships:

- Head: 45 percent of total character height
- Torso: 40 percent of total character height
- Feet and lower spacing: 8 percent
- Crown tuft: 10 to 12 percent above the head mass
- Eye width: approximately 16 to 18 percent of head width per eye
- Beak width: approximately 20 percent of head width
- Wing length: approximately 55 percent of torso height

The head should feel large enough for expressive eyes but must not turn Rook into a baby character.

## Face

### Eyes

- Warm ivory sclera
- Deep plum pupils
- Controlled highlights
- Direct or near-direct eye contact
- Mild asymmetry between the two eyes
- Relaxed upper eyelids
- Calm, attentive expression

The eyes should be smaller and calmer than the earliest Rook concepts. Avoid enormous circular eyes that dominate the entire face.

### Eyebrow feather

One raised lavender eyebrow feather is a permanent signature feature. It communicates curiosity and should remain visible at small sizes.

It must not resemble a human eyebrow pasted onto the face. It should read as a stylized feather form.

### Beak

- Short
- Rounded
- Dark plum
- Soft downward curve
- Slight separation between upper and lower beak
- Subtle closed-beak smile

Avoid long, sharp, realistic, aggressive, or predatory beaks. Do not add human teeth or lips.

## Wings, tail, and feet

Wings should be built from two or three broad shapes. They should behave like expressive arms during animation without becoming human hands.

The resting pose keeps both wings near the body. One may rest lightly on the architectural ledge.

The tail contains three broad geometric feather points. Feet are tiny, rounded, and simple. Avoid claws and realistic bird anatomy.

## Color palette

| Element | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep plum | `#35213F` | Outer body, deep shadows |
| Main purple | `#6846A5` | Primary feather color |
| Dusty lavender | `#9475C4` | Secondary feathers and eyebrow accent |
| Light lavender | `#B9A3DE` | Belly and soft highlights |
| Warm ivory | `#FFF5DD` | Eyes and light accents |
| Charcoal purple | `#251D2C` | Beak detail and maximum contrast |

Avoid pure black, pure white, neon colors, bright green, and rainbow accents.

## Rendering style

- Polished 2D cartoon mascot
- Clean rounded contours
- Broad geometric feather groups
- Minimal interior detail
- Restrained flat shading
- Subtle dimensionality
- Strong small-size legibility
- Animation-ready construction
- Mature editorial finish

Avoid photorealism, realistic feather texture, 3D toy rendering, felt texture, glossy plastic surfaces, rough sketch lines, and excessive gradients.

## Permanent exclusions

Rook must not include:

- Stars on the body
- Spark emblems on the wings
- Printed symbols
- Clothing
- Glasses
- Books
- Graduation caps
- Badges
- Accessories
- Long legs
- Sharp claws
- An open-mouth grin
- A visible tongue
- Owl features
- Green coloring

## Welcome-screen pose

- Seated comfortably on the arch ledge
- Positioned slightly left of center
- Gentle three-quarter body angle
- Direct eye contact
- Relaxed wings
- Tiny feet close together
- Slight head tilt
- Subtle closed-beak smile
- Calm, attentive posture

Rook should look like a companion waiting for the user, not a performer presenting a lesson.

## Required production views

Before final animation, create:

1. Front view
2. Three-quarter left view
3. Side view
4. Three-quarter back view
5. Back view
6. Neutral expression
7. Curious expression
8. Delighted expression
9. Thinking expression
10. Concerned expression
11. Proud expression

All views must use identical proportions, colors, feather grouping, tuft design, beak structure, and eyebrow feather placement.
