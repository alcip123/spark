# Canonical Rook Generation Prompt

Use this prompt to recreate the approved Rook direction for concept development. Production assets should ultimately be drawn and rigged as vectors rather than generated repeatedly.

## Prompt

Create the definitive mascot for Spark, a premium mobile curiosity and knowledge app. The character is Rook, an original stylized purple raven who notices and collects fascinating ideas.

Rook must feel curious, intelligent, observant, comforting, warm, slightly mischievous, and quietly confident. The emotional impression should be: “Rook noticed something fascinating and wants to share it with me.” Rook is a companion, not a teacher, performer, or game host.

Use a polished simplified 2D cartoon mascot style with clean rounded contours, broad geometric feather groups, minimal interior detail, restrained flat shading, subtle dimensionality, strong small-size readability, and animation-ready construction. The design should feel credible for a premium consumer reading or wellness product used by teenagers and adults.

Use a compact pear-shaped body and large rounded head with soft asymmetry. The head occupies approximately 45 percent of total character height. Add a three-part crown tuft with one off-center feather and one raised lavender eyebrow feather. These are permanent identifying features.

Give Rook warm ivory eyes with deep plum pupils, controlled highlights, direct eye contact, relaxed upper eyelids, and mild asymmetry. The expression is attentive, calm, and pleased to see the user. Keep the eyes expressive but controlled. Avoid enormous baby-like eyes.

Use a short rounded dark-plum beak with a soft downward curve and subtle closed-beak smile. Do not show teeth or tongue. Do not use a long, sharp, realistic, or aggressive beak.

Use compact folded crescent-like wings constructed from two or three broad shapes. Use tiny rounded feet and a visible three-point geometric tail. Avoid realistic feathers, claws, long legs, or detailed anatomy.

Color palette: deep plum `#35213F`, main purple `#6846A5`, dusty lavender `#9475C4`, light lavender `#B9A3DE`, warm ivory `#FFF5DD`, and charcoal purple `#251D2C`.

For the welcome-screen pose, seat Rook comfortably on a ledge. Place the body in a gentle three-quarter angle while maintaining direct eye contact. Keep both wings relaxed, with one wing resting lightly near the ledge. Keep the feet close together. Use a slight head tilt and subtle closed-beak smile. Rook should look like a quiet companion waiting for the user.

Do not add stars, spark emblems, printed symbols, wing markings, clothing, glasses, books, graduation caps, badges, accessories, magical particles, floating objects, or props. Do not make Rook green. Do not make Rook an owl. Do not resemble Duo or another existing mascot. Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, plush-toy styling, felt texture, Gothic imagery, open-mouth grins, childish preschool styling, and exaggerated poses.

Render one full-body character on a transparent background with generous padding. Preserve a clean silhouette that remains recognizable at 32 by 32 pixels. No text, labels, logos, interface elements, scenery, borders, or watermarks.
